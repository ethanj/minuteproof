# Rights and access

This repository is a research and workshop package, not a blanket grant of rights to archival material.

## What the manifests mean

Every curated property folder contains a `manifest.json` recording:

- the original source URL;
- local filename and byte size;
- PDF page count;
- SHA-256 checksum;
- download status;
- linked collections that are not available as bulk downloads.

## Important distinctions

- **Publicly accessible** does not always mean **public domain**.
- A finding aid describes a collection; it does not grant possession of every underlying item.
- Some institutions allow research access but require separate permission for reproduction or publication.
- The Manhyia material should remain institution-led and culturally sensitive. Colonial-era contextual records are included as historical evidence to interrogate, not as neutral interpretation.
- Casa Azul’s Smithsonian Weatherwax collection contains 805 digitized images, but the institution does not offer a bulk package. The local folder contains public finding aids and publications, while its manifest links to the item-level collection.

Before publishing images or document excerpts outside the workshop, verify the terms at the original institution linked in the relevant manifest.
