# San Francisco 1906 + Haas-Lilienthal messy document archive

Raw document inputs for a Reducto MCP/API workshop. The collection moves from the citywide earthquake and fire to a detailed case file on the Haas-Lilienthal House at 2007 Franklin Street.

## Collection size

- 194 source files
- 0.51 GB total
- No normalized Markdown or OCR text in the processing folders

## Why this corpus is difficult

- Multi-column historic newspapers with poor scan quality
- Government forms with stamps, checkboxes, handwriting, and skew
- Long illustrated books with foldouts and irregular typography
- Fire-insurance maps with color, dense labels, and handwritten-style notation
- High-resolution TIFF house photographs and caption sheets
- Archival letters and reports with inconsistent formatting
- JPEG, TIFF, PDF, CSV, and GeoJSON inputs in one collection

## Suggested narrative

Start with the 1906 disaster at city scale, locate the burned area and relief activity, then zoom to Franklin Street and reconstruct the Haas-Lilienthal House case file. Treat "why it survived" as a research question rather than a predetermined causal claim.

## MCP path

Use the local MCP server for this folder. Upload one file first, inspect the parse in Studio, then expand to a selected subset. The full Sanborn atlas and high-resolution HABS TIFFs are deliberately large stress-test inputs.

## Rights

See `manifest.csv` or `manifest.json` for the source record and rights note for every file. Most Library of Congress books and Sanborn maps are public domain. LOC photographs were filtered to items marked "No known restrictions on publication." Review embedded photo credits and UC Berkeley/NPS item notes before publishing social content.
